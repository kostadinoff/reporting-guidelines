# GREET

17 items for reporting **educational interventions that teach evidence-based practice**.
Built on the TIDieR structure (`tidier-2014.md`) and using its headings — Brief name, Why,
What, Who provided, How, Where, When and how much, Planned/unplanned changes, How well —
but recast for teaching rather than clinical intervention.

What GREET adds beyond TIDieR: **item 2** (the educational theory underpinning the
intervention), **item 3** (learning objectives), **item 4** (which of the five EBP steps —
ask, acquire, appraise, apply, assess — the intervention covers), **item 7** (learner
incentives), and **item 9's** learner-to-instructor ratio.

Read with the GREET 2015 explanation and elaboration paper, and with the TIDieR guidance.

Cite as `[@phillips2016greet]`.

## Brief name

### Item 1 — Intervention

Provide a brief description of the educational intervention for all groups involved [e.g. control and comparator(s)].

## Why — this educational process

### Item 2 — Theory

Describe the educational theory(ies), concept or approach used in the intervention.

### Item 3 — Learning objectives

Describe the learning objectives for all groups involved in the educational intervention.

### Item 4 — EBP content

List the foundation steps of EBP (ask, acquire, appraise, apply, assess) included in the educational intervention.

## What

### Item 5 — Materials

Describe the specific educational materials used in the educational intervention. Include materials provided to the learners and those used in the training of educational intervention providers.

### Item 6 — Educational strategies

Describe the teaching / learning strategies (e.g. tutorials, lectures, online modules) used in the educational intervention.

### Item 7 — Incentives

Describe any incentives or reimbursements provided to the learners.

## Who provided

### Item 8 — Instructors

For each instructor(s) involved in the educational intervention describe their professional discipline, teaching experience / expertise. Include any specific training related to the educational intervention provided for the instructor(s).

## How

### Item 9 — Delivery

Describe the modes of delivery (e.g. face-to-face, internet or independent study package) of the educational intervention. Include whether the intervention was provided individually or in a group and the ratio of learners to instructors.

## Where

### Item 10 — Environment

Describe the relevant physical learning spaces (e.g. conference, university lecture theatre, hospital ward, community) where the teaching / learning occurred.

## When and how much

### Item 11 — Schedule

Describe the scheduling of the educational intervention including the number of sessions, their frequency, timing and duration.

### Item 12

Describe the amount of time learners spent in face-to-face contact with instructors and any designated time spent in self-directed learning activities.

## Planned changes

### Item 13

Did the educational intervention require specific adaptation for the learners? If yes, please describe the adaptations made for the learner(s) or group(s).

## Unplanned changes

### Item 14

Was the educational intervention modified during the course of the study? If yes, describe the changes (what, why, when, and how).

## How well

### Item 15 — Attendance

Describe the learner attendance, including how this was assessed and by whom. Describe any strategies that were used to facilitate attendance.

### Item 16

Describe any processes used to determine whether the materials (item 5) and the educational strategies (item 6) used in the educational intervention were delivered as originally planned.

### Item 17

Describe the extent to which the number of sessions, their frequency, timing and duration for the educational intervention was delivered as scheduled (item 11).
