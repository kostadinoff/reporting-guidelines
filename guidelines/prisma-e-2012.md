# PRISMA-E 2012

Equity extensions to PRISMA. Only the equity-specific additions are reproduced here; the
base items are in `prisma-2020.md`.

**Version caveat.** Written against PRISMA 2009, so item numbers refer to that version. Map
by topic onto PRISMA 2020.

The organising idea is **PROGRESS-Plus** — place of residence, race/ethnicity, occupation,
gender, religion, education, socioeconomic status, social capital, plus other factors — as
the axes along which a review should examine differential effects. The distinctive
requirements are **3** and **3A** (state the assumed mechanism by which the intervention
affects equity, and give the logic model), **14** (present both relative *and* absolute
differences between groups — a relative effect can look equitable while the absolute gap
widens), and **26** (limits of applicability to disadvantaged populations, with the evidence
and logic behind that judgment).

Cite as `[@welch2012prismae]`.

## Title

### Item 1 — Title (extension)

Identify equity as a focus of the review, if relevant, using the term equity.

## Abstract

### Item 2 — Structured summary (extension)

State research question(s) related to health equity.

### Item 2A — Structured summary

Present results of health equity analyses (e.g. subgroup analyses or meta-regression).

### Item 2B — Structured summary

Describe extent and limits of applicability to disadvantaged populations of interest.

## Introduction

### Item 3 — Rationale (extension)

Describe assumptions about mechanism(s) by which the intervention is assumed to have an impact on health equity.

### Item 3A — Rationale

Provide the logic model/analytical framework, if done, to show the pathways through which the intervention is assumed to affect health equity and how it was developed.

### Item 4 — Objectives (extension)

Describe how disadvantage was defined if used as criterion in the review (e.g. for selecting studies, conducting analyses or judging applicability).

### Item 4A — Objectives

State the research questions being addressed with reference to health equity.

## Methods

### Item 6 — Eligibility criteria (extension)

Describe the rationale for including particular study designs related to equity research questions.

### Item 6A — Eligibility criteria

Describe the rationale for including the outcomes — e.g. how these are relevant to reducing inequity.

### Item 7 — Information sources (extension)

Describe information sources (e.g. health, non-health, and grey literature sources) that were searched that are of specific relevance to address the equity questions of the review.

### Item 8 — Search (extension)

Describe the broad search strategy and terms used to address equity questions of the review.

### Item 11 — Data items (extension)

List and define data items related to equity, where such data were sought (e.g. using PROGRESS-Plus or other criteria, context).

### Item 14 — Synthesis of results (extension)

Describe methods of synthesizing findings on health inequities (e.g. presenting both relative and absolute differences between groups).

### Item 16 — Additional analyses (extension)

Describe methods of additional synthesis approaches related to equity questions, if done, indicating which were pre-specified.

## Results

### Item 18 — Study characteristics (extension)

Present the population characteristics that relate to the equity questions across the relevant PROGRESS-Plus or other factors of interest.

### Item 21 — Synthesis of results (extension)

Present the results of synthesizing findings on inequities (see item 14).

### Item 23 — Additional analysis (extension)

Give the results of additional synthesis approaches related to equity objectives, if done (see item 16).

## Discussion

### Item 26 — Conclusions (extension)

Present extent and limits of applicability to disadvantaged populations of interest and describe the evidence and logic underlying those judgments.

### Item 26A — Conclusions

Provide implications for research, practice or policy related to equity where relevant (e.g. types of research needed to address unanswered questions).
