# SPIRIT-AI

Extension items only. The base protocol items are in `spirit-2025.md`.

**Version caveat.** SPIRIT-AI was published against **SPIRIT 2013**; the item numbers below
refer to that version. SPIRIT 2025 has since been published and harmonised with CONSORT
2025, so map these by topic rather than by number.

**Source note.** The copy transcribed here came from a *completed* checklist filed as
supplementary material with a specific trial protocol (Kelshiker et al., BMJ Open 2025;
15:e098030), so the original carried that trial's page numbers. Only the checklist item text
is reproduced — the trial-specific page references are omitted.

Items are marked **Elaboration** (clarifying an existing SPIRIT item) or **Extension**
(adding a new requirement). Mirrors `consort-ai-2020.md` for the trial report.

## Administrative information — SPIRIT 2013 item 1

### Item 1 (i) — Elaboration

Indicate that the intervention involves artificial intelligence / machine learning and specify the type of model.

### Item 1 (ii) — Elaboration

Specify the intended use of the AI intervention.

## Introduction — SPIRIT 2013 item 6a

### Item 6a (i) — Extension

Explain the intended use of the AI intervention in the context of the clinical pathway, including its purpose and its intended users (e.g. healthcare professionals, patients, public).

### Item 6a (ii) — Extension

Describe any pre-existing evidence for the AI intervention.

## Methods: study setting — SPIRIT 2013 item 9

### Item 9 — Extension

Describe the onsite and offsite requirements needed to integrate the AI intervention into the trial setting.

## Methods: eligibility criteria — SPIRIT 2013 item 10

### Item 10 (i) — Elaboration

State the inclusion and exclusion criteria at the level of participants.

### Item 10 (ii) — Extension

State the inclusion and exclusion criteria at the level of the input data.

## Methods: interventions — SPIRIT 2013 item 11a

### Item 11a (i) — Extension

State which version of the AI algorithm will be used.

### Item 11a (ii) — Extension

Specify the procedure for acquiring and selecting the input data for the AI intervention.

### Item 11a (iii) — Extension

Specify the procedure for assessing and handling poor quality or unavailable input data.

### Item 11a (iv) — Extension

Specify whether there is human-AI interaction in the handling of the input data, and what level of expertise is required for users.

### Item 11a (v) — Extension

Specify the output of the AI intervention.

### Item 11a (vi) — Extension

Explain the procedure for how the AI intervention's output will contribute to decision-making or other elements of clinical practice.

## Methods: harms — SPIRIT 2013 item 22

### Item 22 — Extension

Specify any plans to identify and analyse performance errors. If there are no plans for this, explain why not.

## Ethics and dissemination: access to data — SPIRIT 2013 item 29

### Item 29 — Extension

State whether and how the AI intervention and/or its code can be accessed, including any restrictions to access or re-use.
