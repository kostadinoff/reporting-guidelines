# TRIPOD+AI for Abstracts

13 items for the abstract of a prediction-model study. **TRIPOD+AI item 2 defers to this
checklist**, so `tripod-ai-2024.md` is incomplete without it — the same relationship PRISMA
2020 has with `prisma-abstracts-2020.md`.

Applies to conference abstracts as well as journal abstracts.

Cite as `[@collins2024tripodai]` (published as Table 3 of the TRIPOD+AI statement).

> **Incomplete footnote.** Items 7 and 10 carry a `†` marker in the published table. The
> footnote text defining it was not captured in transcription — check the source before
> relying on those two items. Everything else is verbatim.

## Title

### Item 1

Identify the study as developing or evaluating the performance of a multivariable prediction model, the target population, and the outcome to be predicted.

## Background

### Item 2

Provide a brief explanation of the healthcare context and rationale for developing or evaluating the performance of all models.

## Objectives

### Item 3

Specify the study objectives, including whether the study describes model development, evaluation, or both.

## Methods

### Item 4

Describe the sources of data.

### Item 5

Describe the eligibility criteria and setting where the data were collected.

### Item 6

Specify the outcome to be predicted by the model, including time horizon of predictions in case of prognostic models.

### Item 7

Specify the type of model, a summary of the model-building steps, and the method for internal validation†.

### Item 8

Specify the measures used to assess model performance (eg, discrimination, calibration, clinical utility).

## Results

### Item 9

Report the number of participants and outcome events.

### Item 10

Summarise the predictors in the final model†.

### Item 11

Report model performance estimates (with confidence intervals).

## Discussion

### Item 12

Give an overall interpretation of the main results.

## Registration

### Item 13

Give the registration number and name of the registry or repository.
