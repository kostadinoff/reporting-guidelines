# CONSORT-Outcomes 2022

17 sub-items elaborating how **outcomes** are defined, measured, analysed and reported.
Attaches to CONSORT 2010 items 6a, 7a, 12a, 17a and 18; base items are in `consort-2025.md`.

**Version caveat.** Written against CONSORT 2010, so item numbers refer to that version.
CONSORT 2025 restructured the checklist — map by topic.

Ten of the seventeen hang off item 6a alone, which is the point: "completely defined
prespecified primary and secondary outcome measures" turns out to require the measurement
variable, the analysis metric, the method of aggregation and the time point (**6a.2**) —
the four elements that make an outcome actually specified. **6a.7** is the integrity item:
identify any outcome *not* prespecified in a registry or protocol.

Pairs with `tidier-2014.md`, which CONSORT item 5 defers to for the intervention.

Cite as `[@butcher2022consortoutcomes]`.

## Methods: outcomes — CONSORT item 6a

### Item 6a.1

Provide a rationale for the selection of the domain for the trial's primary outcome.

### Item 6a.2

Describe the specific measurement variable (eg, systolic blood pressure), analysis metric (eg, change from baseline, final value, time to event), method of aggregation (eg, mean, proportion), and the time point for each outcome.

### Item 6a.3

If the analysis metric for the primary outcome represents within-participant change, define and justify the minimal important change in individuals.

### Item 6a.4

If the outcome data were continuous, but were analyzed as categorical (method of aggregation), specify the cutoff values used.

### Item 6a.5

If outcome assessments were performed at several time points after randomization, state the time points used for the analysis.

### Item 6a.6

If a composite outcome was used, define all individual components of the composite outcome.

### Item 6a.7

Identify any outcomes that were not prespecified in a trial registry or trial protocol.

### Item 6a.8

Provide a description of the study instruments used to assess the outcome (eg, questionnaires, laboratory tests) along with reliability, validity, and responsiveness in a population similar to the study sample.

### Item 6a.9

Describe who assessed the outcome (eg, nurse, parent) and any qualifications or trial-specific training necessary to administer the study instruments to assess the outcome.

### Item 6a.10

Describe any processes used to promote outcome data quality during data collection (eg, duplicate measurements) and after data collection (eg, range checks of outcome data values), or state where these details can be found.

## Methods: sample size — CONSORT item 7a

### Item 7a.1

Define and justify the target difference between treatment groups (eg, the minimal important difference).

## Methods: statistical methods — CONSORT item 12a

### Item 12a.1

Describe any methods used to account for multiplicity in the analysis or interpretation of the primary and secondary outcomes (eg, coprimary outcomes, same outcome assessed at multiple time points, or subgroup analyses of an outcome).

### Item 12a.2

State and justify any criteria for excluding any outcome data from the analysis and reporting, or report that no outcome data were excluded.

### Item 12a.3

Describe the methods used to assess patterns of missingness (eg, missing not at random), and describe the methods used to handle missing outcome items or entire assessments.

### Item 12a.4

Provide a definition of the outcome analysis population relating to nonadherence of the trial protocol (eg, as a randomized analysis).

## Results: outcomes and estimation — CONSORT item 17a

### Item 17a.1

Include the results for all prespecified outcome analyses or state where the results can be found if not in this report.

## Results: ancillary analyses — CONSORT item 18

### Item 18.1

If there were any analyses that were not prespecified, explain why they were performed.
