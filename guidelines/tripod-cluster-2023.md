# TRIPOD-Cluster

19 items for prediction models developed or validated on **clustered data** — individual
participant data meta-analysis, multi-centre cohorts, models built across hospitals or
countries. Referenced directly by TRIPOD+AI items 12d and 23b, which defer to it whenever
heterogeneity across clusters is in play.

The whole point is that a model's performance is not one number when the data are clustered.
Hence **8c** and **8f** (how heterogeneity in parameter values and in performance was handled
and quantified), **13a** (performance reported overall *and for each cluster*), and **13b**
(results of heterogeneity assessment). A single pooled c-statistic hides exactly what this
checklist exists to surface.

Items marked with an asterisk in the source adapt one or more items from the original TRIPOD
checklist.

Cite as `[@debray2023tripodcluster]`.

## Title and abstract

### Item 1

Identify the study as developing and/or validating a multivariable prediction model, the target population, and the outcome to be predicted.

### Item 2

Provide a summary of research objectives, setting, participants, data source, sample size, predictors, outcome, statistical analysis, results, and conclusions.

## Introduction

### Item 3a

Explain the medical context (including whether diagnostic or prognostic) and rationale for developing or validating the prediction model, including references to existing models, and the advantages of the study design.

### Item 3b

Specify the objectives, including whether the study describes the development or validation of the model.

## Methods

### Item 4a

Describe eligibility criteria for participants and datasets.

### Item 4b

Describe the origin of the data, and how the data were identified, requested, and collected.

### Item 5

Explain how the sample size was arrived at.

### Item 6a

Define the outcome that is predicted by the model, including how and when assessed.

### Item 6b

Define all predictors used in developing or validating the model, including how and when measured.

### Item 7a

Describe how the data were prepared for analysis, including any cleaning, harmonisation, linkage, and quality checks.

### Item 7b

Describe the method for assessing risk of bias and applicability in the individual clusters (eg, using PROBAST).

### Item 7c

For validation, identify any differences in definition and measurement from the development data (eg, setting, eligibility criteria, outcome, predictors).

### Item 7d

Describe how missing data were handled.

### Item 8a

Describe how predictors were handled in the analyses.

### Item 8b

Specify the type of model, all model-building procedures (eg, any predictor selection and penalisation), and method for validation.

### Item 8c

Describe how any heterogeneity across clusters (eg, studies or settings) in model parameter values was handled.

### Item 8d

For validation, describe how the predictions were calculated.

### Item 8e

Specify all measures used to assess model performance (eg, calibration, discrimination, and decision curve analysis) and, if relevant, to compare multiple models.

### Item 8f

Describe how any heterogeneity across clusters (eg, studies or settings) in model performance was handled and quantified.

### Item 8g

Describe any model updating (eg, recalibration) arising from the validation, either overall or for particular populations or settings.

### Item 9

Describe any planned subgroup or sensitivity analysis (eg, assessing performance according to sources of bias, participant characteristics, setting).

## Results

### Item 10a

Describe the number of clusters and participants from data identified through to data analysed. A flow chart may be helpful.

### Item 10b

Report the characteristics overall and where applicable for each data source or setting, including the key dates, predictors, treatments received, sample size, number of outcome events, follow-up time, and amount of missing data.

### Item 10c

For validation, show a comparison with the development data of the distribution of important variables (demographics, predictors, and outcome).

### Item 11

Report the results of the risk of bias assessment in the individual clusters.

### Item 12a

Report the results of any across-cluster heterogeneity assessments that led to subsequent actions during the model's development (eg, inclusion or exclusion of particular predictors or clusters).

### Item 12b

Present the final prediction model (ie, all regression coefficients, and model intercept or baseline estimate of the outcome at a given time point) and explain how to use it for predictions in new individuals.

### Item 13a

Report performance measures (with uncertainty intervals) for the prediction model, overall and for each cluster.

### Item 13b

Report results of any heterogeneity across clusters in model performance.

### Item 14

Report the results from any model updating (including the updated model equation and subsequent performance), overall and for each cluster.

### Item 15

Report results from any subgroup or sensitivity analysis.

## Discussion

### Item 16a

Give an overall interpretation of the main results, including heterogeneity across clusters in model performance, in the context of the objectives and previous studies.

### Item 16b

For validation, discuss the results with reference to the model performance in the development data, and in any previous validations.

### Item 16c

Discuss the strengths of the study and any limitations (eg, missing or incomplete data, non-representativeness, data harmonisation problems).

### Item 17

Discuss the potential use of the model and implications for future research, with specific view to generalisability and applicability of the model across different settings or (sub)populations.

## Other information

### Item 18

Provide information about the availability of supplementary resources (eg, study protocol, analysis code, datasets).

### Item 19

Give the source of funding and the role of the funders for the present study.
