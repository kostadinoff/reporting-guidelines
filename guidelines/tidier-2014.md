# TIDieR

12 items describing **what an intervention actually was**, in enough detail to replicate it.

**Not a standalone guideline.** TIDieR expands the intervention-description item of whatever
checklist governs the study design:

- randomised trial → use with CONSORT (extension of CONSORT 2010 item 5)
- trial protocol → use with SPIRIT (extension of SPIRIT 2013 item 11)
- other designs → use with the appropriate design checklist

It covers intervention (and where relevant comparison) elements only. Other methodological
features are deliberately not duplicated from the parent checklist.

Two conventions from the checklist form worth keeping:

- Items **10** and **12** cannot be completed for a *protocol* — they describe modifications
  and actual fidelity, which are only knowable once the study is done.
- Authors mark **N/A** where an item does not apply; reviewers mark **?** where information
  is absent or insufficiently reported. For each item, record where the information sits —
  primary paper (page/appendix) or elsewhere (protocol, another paper, a URL).

Cite as `[@hoffmann2014tidier]`. The TIDieR guide (BMJ 2014;348:g1687) carries explanation
and elaboration for each item.

## Item 1 — Brief name

Provide the name or a phrase that describes the intervention.

## Item 2 — Why

Describe any rationale, theory, or goal of the elements essential to the intervention.

## Item 3 — What (materials)

Describe any physical or informational materials used in the intervention, including those provided to participants or used in intervention delivery or in training of intervention providers. Provide information on where the materials can be accessed (e.g. online appendix, URL).

## Item 4 — What (procedures)

Describe each of the procedures, activities, and/or processes used in the intervention, including any enabling or support activities.

## Item 5 — Who provided

For each category of intervention provider (e.g. psychologist, nursing assistant), describe their expertise, background and any specific training given.

## Item 6 — How

Describe the modes of delivery (e.g. face-to-face or by some other mechanism, such as internet or telephone) of the intervention and whether it was provided individually or in a group.

## Item 7 — Where

Describe the type(s) of location(s) where the intervention occurred, including any necessary infrastructure or relevant features.

## Item 8 — When and how much

Describe the number of times the intervention was delivered and over what period of time including the number of sessions, their schedule, and their duration, intensity or dose.

## Item 9 — Tailoring

If the intervention was planned to be personalised, titrated or adapted, then describe what, why, when, and how.

## Item 10 — Modifications ǂ

If the intervention was modified during the course of the study, describe the changes (what, why, when, and how).

## Item 11 — How well (planned)

If intervention adherence or fidelity was assessed, describe how and by whom, and if any strategies were used to maintain or improve fidelity, describe them.

## Item 12 — How well (actual) ǂ

If intervention adherence or fidelity was assessed, describe the extent to which the intervention was delivered as planned.

## Footnotes

- **ǂ** When completing TIDieR for a *protocol*, items 10 and 12 are not relevant and cannot be described until the study is complete.
- Authors use **N/A** if an item is not applicable to the intervention being described. Reviewers use **?** if information about the element is not reported or not sufficiently reported.
- If information is not in the primary paper, give details of where it is available — a published protocol, another paper (with citation), or a website (with URL).
