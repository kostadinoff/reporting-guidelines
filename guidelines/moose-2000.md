# MOOSE

Reporting proposal for **meta-analyses of observational studies**, where the analytic
problems differ from those of trial meta-analysis: confounding cannot be assumed away by
randomisation, and heterogeneity is the norm rather than the exception.

**Numbering note.** The published checklist is a tick-box form with headed groups and **no
item numbers**. Items are numbered sequentially here so they can be referenced and grepped;
those numbers are this repository's convention, not MOOSE's. Cite by section and item text,
not by number. This transcription yields 34 items; MOOSE is sometimes described as having
35, so a source may split one of these differently.

**Relation to PRISMA.** MOOSE predates PRISMA and is still requested by some journals for
observational meta-analysis. Where a journal does not mandate MOOSE, PRISMA 2020 is
generally the better-maintained choice; the two overlap heavily. MOOSE retains distinctive
emphases on searcher qualifications, hand searching, and non-English-language handling.

Cite as `[@stroup2000moose]`.

## Methods

### Item 17
Description of relevance or appropriateness of studies assembled for assessing the hypothesis to be tested.

### Item 18
Rationale for the selection and coding of data (e.g., sound clinical principles or convenience).

### Item 19
Documentation of how data were classified and coded (e.g., multiple raters, blinding, and interrater reliability).

### Item 20
Assessment of confounding (e.g., comparability of cases and controls in studies where appropriate).

### Item 21
Assessment of study quality, including blinding of quality assessors; stratification or regression on possible predictors of study results.

### Item 22
Assessment of heterogeneity.

### Item 23
Description of statistical methods (e.g., complete description of fixed or random effects models, justification of whether the chosen models account for predictors of study results, dose-response models, or cumulative meta-analysis) in sufficient detail to be replicated.

### Item 24
Provision of appropriate tables and graphics.

## Results

### Item 25
Table giving descriptive information for each study included.

### Item 26
Results of sensitivity testing (e.g., subgroup analysis).

### Item 27
Indication of statistical uncertainty of findings.

## Discussion

### Item 28
Quantitative assessment of bias (e.g., publication bias).

### Item 29
Justification for exclusion (e.g., exclusion of non-English-language citations).

### Item 30
Assessment of quality of included studies.

## Conclusions

### Item 31
Consideration of alternative explanations for observed results.

### Item 32
Generalization of the conclusions (i.e., appropriate for the data presented and within the domain of the literature review).

### Item 33
Guidelines for future research.

### Item 34
Disclosure of funding source.
